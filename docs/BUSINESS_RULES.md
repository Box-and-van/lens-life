# Business rules
- Lens Life platform share: 20%.
- Photographer share: 80% before explicit adjustments.
- Default payout delay: 5 days after verified completion and clean reconciliation.
- Booking confirmation occurs from a trusted payment event, not a success-page redirect.
- Only approved photographers matching service, coverage and declared availability can receive offers.
- Delivery stages are distinct: uploaded → quality review → delivered → customer approved → completed → payout eligible.
- Recurring B2B is the commercial priority and has its own account/site/template/contract workflow.
